Clean Room: the Stats tab replaces the yearly spreadsheet — years side by side, a row per fish, the prices to raise, and what customers really paid (no schema change; one option, one transient). A strip under the tab: **By year** (the tab as it was) · **Compare years** · **Species** · **Prices to raise** · **Real sales**; every view says which numbers are ESTIMATES (survivors × the FisHotel price typed on the supplier order line, as the spreadsheet did — now headed "Est." on By year too) and which are ACTUAL. **Compare years**: tick two or more years (the last two with orders by default, at most six); for the totals and for each supplier, origin and species group a row per year with the weighted figures — orders, ordered, received, fill, survived, survival, DOA and its rate, landed, est. revenue, est. up / down — and under it the change from the year before: counts and money as the difference with the percent where one is defined (never from zero), rates in points, up / down in money only. A supplier, origin or group with no order in a year shows a dash there, never a zero, and no change into or out of that year. **Species**: one row per fish and year — orders, ordered, received, DOA, survived, landed total and per fish, est. revenue, est. profit and est. margin — for a year or every year, with a search and every heading sortable; a fish is its species-list row (a line typed with a row's exact name counts for it), the fish not identified yet are one row, so the rows of a year add up to that year's totals; a fish without a survivor count says so instead of showing a loss. **Prices to raise** (read-only — no price is ever changed): per fish with a received, priced supplier line, the landed cost per fish of the latest order and the plain average of the last three, the suggested price exactly as the costing computes it (landed per fish × that order's markup, to the nearest 5), FisHotel's price — the linked product's current price when it has one (for a product with variations, the variation of the size the fish came in, else the lowest), else the species list's — the gap and the margin; the list shows the fish priced under the suggestion, biggest gap first, with group and supplier filters (a supplier filter costs the fish from that supplier's orders alone) and a "show all" toggle. **Real sales**: per fish and year, units sold, actual revenue, the average price paid beside today's list price, the estimate beside the actual, and the actual profit = actual revenue − units sold × that year's average landed cost per fish — a fish that sold in a year in which none landed gets no profit figure, the cell says "no landed cost in <year>" (with the last earlier cost for reference). Read from WooCommerce's order-product lookup table joined to its order stats (so it works with HPOS on or off), for orders in a status that counts — WooCommerce's paid statuses plus every registered status whose slug contains "ship", editable under Settings → *Sales statuses* —, net of refunds (a refund comes off the line and the year of the order it refunds; money refunded without naming a line is shown apart). A line is a fish by its product (a species linked to that product, every variation included), by its line in the Invoices section when that section is active (`fishotel_invoices_for_order()`, or the filter `fishotel_cleanroom_sales_invoice_lines`), or by an exact lower-cased name — the species name, the title of its linked product, a learned alias — for the theme's itemised invoice lines (`_fh_invoice_fish`) and for the "Includes" snapshot (`_fh_invoice_lines`) of an invoice kept as one line, which is split only when its lines add up to what was paid, to the cent. Everything else is listed under **Not matched** with its units and its money, by kind and with what would get it matched, so matched + not matched is always every paid line and nothing is guessed. A year is read in bounded, prepared pages, kept in one transient for twelve hours, and dropped when an order changes status, when WooCommerce updates its order tables, and by the **Refresh** button; nothing runs outside the Stats tab, and an ordinary request still costs no query. **Exports**: XLSX / CSV of all four views through the existing export action (`compare`, `species-years`, `prices`, `sales`); an empty cell means "no figure", never a 0; the Real sales CSV is one table that carries the "not matched" too (a leading `section` column — the fish and one row per kind of not matched add up to every paid line). **Holding up over the years and on a bad day**: Compare years and the landed costs beside Real sales read each year by itself, as By year does, so nothing drops off once there are more than 500 supplier orders (Prices to raise keeps to the newest 500 — a fish's latest order and its last three — and says so on the page when there are more); the Species rows of a group add up to that group on By year (a line only typed with a species' name is not linked to it, so it is under no group in both — pick the fish from the list and it joins its group); a database read that fails part-way is never taken for the end of the year — the page says every figure is INCOMPLETE and that reading is not cached, so reopening the page reads the year again; and for invoice orders from before invoices were itemised ("no fish lines recorded on the order") the page names the theme's Tools button that records them and drops the cache when that tool runs. Headless tests: stats-test (the comparison maths, the dash for a missing year, the per-species totals), stats-prices-test (the selection, the rounding to the nearest 5, the price source order, read-only), stats-sales-test (a fake of the lookup tables: refunds, a variation rolled up to its species, statuses in and out, the Invoices section absent and present, the exact-name matching, "not matched" adding up, the cache) and stats-admin-test (every screen rendered, the exports, 600 orders against the 500-order read, a failed read).
