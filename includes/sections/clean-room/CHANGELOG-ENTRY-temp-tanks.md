Clean Room: temporary tanks and one to-do list for the day, by water system (schema 1.6 through the autoloaded version gate, which now also waits for an added ENUM value; upgrades from 1.3, 1.4 and 1.5 keep every row). **Temporary tanks** — hospital tanks and the like: a building "Temporary" (900, flagged `own_water`, area `temp`) whose tanks are EACH THEIR OWN WATER and are numbered slots 901–909 (hundreds = building, tens 0 = not on a rack, ones = the slot); six slots are added with the upgrade, Settings → Layout says how many there are and adds one more with a click (nine at most — the ones digit is the slot), and *Print tank labels* prints a label per slot ("TEMP 1 · 901") to laminate once and reuse: a slot's label always opens whatever is set up in it, and "Nothing is set up here — Set up" while it is free. **Setting one up** (Board → *Set up a temporary tank*, the phone's tank picker, or a free slot's own label): the slot (the first free one), a name, what it is for, the kind — **Quarantine** (behaves as a Spa tank: arrivals may go in, fish in it stay uncleared, a move from it into a clean tank clears the group) or **Clean** (behaves as a Clean Room tank: no direct arrivals, a quarantine group moved into it is cleared — the Move sheet says so and asks first) —, the gallons, the treatment and its start date, a note. **What it is for → the treatment**: every schedule template gains a "Used for" field (short words; only Trifecta is seeded, with "bacterial infection" — the rest are left for the owner); choosing a word offers the templates carrying it and shows, before anything is saved, which medications on which days; any template, or "no treatment, just watching", can be chosen directly. The chosen template's steps are **copied** into the setup: the tank runs its own schedule (changed for this tank alone on the phone or the Board, never touching the template; a later template edit never reaches a running tank) on its **own clock** — days count from its treatment start date, not from a fish's check-in or a building — and a fish's own quarantine day and dates are untouched. Readings, doses and ticks on a temporary tank are that tank's own water (the Dose sheet's "this tank only", with the setup's gallons); a slot with nothing set up takes no fish and no entry. Moves gain the reason chip *hospital*. **Taking it down** (refused while fish are in it — "move them out first") closes the setup and frees the slot; it is kept in a history — name, purpose, kind, gallons, who and when, the treatment as it was run with what was ticked, the doses and readings, the groups that passed through — and the next setup in that slot starts clean. What a slot was when a group moved rides on the move event, so the same slot can be quarantine this month and clean the next without changing anybody's cleared date. **To do** — a new tab after Board, and *Today* on the phone's tank picker: one block per quarantine building — the whole shared system: its name, gallons, schedule and day — and one per temporary tank (marked); in a building's block every due dose, test and water change appears once for the system, never once per tank (groups on different days: each step once, saying which day's group it is for), with the medication and the dose it names and its note; it is ticked once for the whole system — on the list, on any tank's own screen, by a dose, or by a copper reading taken on any tank of the building — and every tank's screen and the Board then show it ticked; the feeding collapses to one line ("Feed — 7 tanks with fish") that opens to the tanks and can be ticked all at once; at the top the number of things left, the copper flags (below the floor, due, overdue) and "All done".
