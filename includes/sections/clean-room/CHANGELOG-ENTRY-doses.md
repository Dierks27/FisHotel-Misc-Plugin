Clean Room: a dose calculator for copper, Metronidazole and every other medication, and the working in the log (no table and no schema change — the list lives in the section's settings option). **Medications** are an editable list now (Settings → Medications, `manage_options`), seeded from the fixed one — the same keys, names, other names and rates — so nothing changes until it is edited: a name, a chip for the phone, other names (for speech, log notes and schedule steps), a unit (mg, g, ml, tsp, drops, each) and how it is dosed — *per gallon* (an amount for so many gallons: 25 mg per gallon, 1 ml per 10 gal), *to a level* (an amount that raises a reading by 1.0, with the reading's unit: the copper product at 0.692 ml per gallon per 1.0 ppm — the row whose key is `copper`, the one the copper log tracks) or *typed* (no sum: Salt); optional **named doses** (Chloroquine's Loading 40, Top-up 12 and Last 6 mg per gallon, the three the seeded CQ schedule uses) and a **measure** ("one scoop = 0.5 g", so a result also reads in scoops). Add, edit, retire and bring back — never delete: a retired medication is no longer offered but the logs that name it still read, a renamed one keeps its old name as an other name for the same reason, two medications cannot share a name, and every refusal says what is wrong. Beside the copper rate the panel shows the figure the store's public Medication Calculator uses when the theme's data is there (through the filter `fishotel_cleanroom_theme_medication_data`, else the theme's own class — read defensively, never depended on): "the same figure", or both and that one is out of date; and says plainly when it is not available. **The Dose sheet** (Front Desk) does the sum for any of them: the water — the whole system by default (the building's system gallons) or this tank only —, then per gallon "Add 2.5 g Metronidazole" with its working small under it ("100 gal × 25 mg"), the rate being the medication's own, a named-dose chip, or the rate of a schedule step due today that names it ("Chloroquine 40 mg/gal", marked "due today"); to a level two numbers — **Now** (for the copper product today's latest copper reading for that water, with its time; a reading on any tank of the building reads the system, yesterday's does not count; otherwise empty and asked for) and **Bring it to** (the level a due step names — "Copper Power to 2.0" on day 1, "to 2.4" on day 2 —, else the copper target of Settings; always editable) — "Add 16.6 ml Copper Power" ("100 gal × 0.24 ppm × 0.692"), and "Nothing to add" at or above the target; a **Top-up** switch for after a water change ("I changed __ % of the water", or gallons): a per-gallon medication is summed for the water that was replaced, one dosed to a level stays Now → Bring it to. One decimal from 1 up and two below; a weight reads in grams from 1 g up and in milligrams below (2,500 mg reads 2.5 g). The amount field is filled with the result and stays editable, because what was really added is what is logged; unknown gallons are said and the amount is typed. **Save** logs once: the dose through the existing dose log (medication, amount, unit, system or tank) with a note carrying the working — "2.16 → 2.40 ppm, 100 gal", "25 mg per gallon, 100 gal", "Loading: 40 mg per gallon, 100 gal", "… · top-up after a 25 % water change" —, built on the server from the store's gallons; for the copper product also the copper reading (the Now value, on the same water) unless it is today's latest reading there already; the existing rules then tick the day's matching to-do steps, the toast says all of it, and a dose still has no undo — the sheet says so before Save, and it (and a phrase's preview) says when that medication went into that water today already ("Already logged today: Copper Power 16.6 ml, at 09:20."), asking for a new reading first when copper was dosed after the last one; nothing is refused. A plain typed dose logs exactly what it always did. **Calculator**, without logging: on the phone (the tank screen, the tank picker, `/?fh_front_desk=1&calc=1`) and on the Board (a button in the toolbar, each building's water one click away) — a medication and any number of gallons give the same result and working; nothing is saved. **By voice or typed phrase**: "copper 2.16 to 2.4", "bring copper from 2.16 to 2.4", "raise copper to 2.4" (today's latest reading is Now; asked for when there is none), "dose metro" / "metro for the system" / "prazi this tank" / "… the 100 gallon system", a named dose ("cq loading") → a preview with the result line and its working, never auto-confirmed, then Confirm does the same Save; a dose spoken with its amount ("metro 5 g") is logged as said, without a sum; a phrase with anything but dose words in it is read as before — "521 copper 2.16", "copper 2.4" and "copper to 2.4" stay plain readings and the existing parser test passes unchanged. **Afterwards** the working is in the log's own note: the Events tab line and the Board's "last dose" for the building show it ("Copper Power 16.6 ml (2.16 → 2.40 ppm, 100 gal)"), and anything that lists a dose's note carries it. The sum is written twice on purpose — `Dose::plan()` on the server, `js/dose-math.js` in the pages — and held together by one fixture both sides' tests read, with the rounding spelled out so PHP and JavaScript agree to the last digit. Headless tests for the list (the seed equals the old fixed list; add, edit, retire, validation; old logs still readable; the theme figure present and absent), every way of dosing and its rounding, named doses, a schedule step's rate, the top-up, zero and negative differences, system versus one tank, unknown gallons, what Now and Bring it to start with (a reading from today, from yesterday, none; day 1, day 2, a day with no copper step), the Save (the dose once, the reading once, no second reading), the phrases, and the sheet, the Calculator and the preview run against a stand-in DOM — invented medications and numbers only, except the two sums asked for by name.
