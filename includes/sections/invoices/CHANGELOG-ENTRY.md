Added Invoices section — a customer's invoice is built from a pick list instead of a hand-made product, and checked against everything that customer already has before it exists. FisHotel Tools → Invoices (`manage_woocommerce`): **New invoice** has one search box that answers in four groups — tank groups from the Clean Room with how many are alive, shop products with their size variations, the species list, and "use what I typed" — a quantity stepper, the price filled in where one is known, credit and note lines, a flat total that leaves a Discount (or Adjustment) line so the lines always add up to the stated price, shipping Premium / Free / another class, a live preview, and a per-line "take from shop stock when paid" tick with a warning when shop stock is short. The **pre-check** is always shown and cannot be skipped: the customer's orders (paid from the paid date, never a status name), all of their invoices including hand-made ones, every fish that already appears on another invoice (each confirmed one by one), and the product exactly as it will be written; Publish needs a pre-check of exactly that content. **Publish** writes the product as the hand-made ones are — title `<Handle> Invoice (n/j/y)`, short description `<Handle> Only`, a `<ul>` description, regular price and never a sale price, stock 1 with backorders off, hidden, the Invoices category only, the shipping class, the image by a threshold from Settings, and the `_fh_invoice_lines` JSON the theme itemises orders from (with `product_id` only on ticked lines) — reads it back, and shows the link with a Copy button. The rules are enforced in code: a published invoice is changed only through the deliberate "Edit this unpaid invoice", refused once it is paid or a live order contains it; more fish go on a new invoice; Trash only when no order contains it, never delete. A paid order marks its invoice paid and fires `fishotel_invoice_paid` (`fishotel_invoice_released` when that order is cancelled or fails). The **forum list** gives `@Handle $Total link` lines (or `[USER=id]Handle[/USER]`) for the ticked invoices or everything published today, with Copy. For other code: `fishotel_invoices_lines_for_product()`, `fishotel_invoices_for_order()`, the filter `fishotel_invoices_search_groups`. Own tables `{prefix}fh_invoices` / `fh_invoice_lines` behind an autoloaded version option; no query on a front-end request; no e-mail, no money and no stock moved by the section. Clean Room, the theme module and Health are reached only when present, and the screen says what is unavailable. Hardened after review: one writer per invoice (a lock on the database server, so a double-clicked Publish makes one product, not several); a window that is behind is refused at the pre-check and at every save and can no longer undo a newer one; a WooCommerce "Duplicate" of an invoice does not carry its mark or its lines and is treated as made by hand; invoices are taken out of the product sitemap, the REST product lists (`wp/v2/product`, the Store API) and the REST search, and their pages are `noindex` — they are found by their link only; `fishotel_invoice_paid` fires once per payment, also when an order goes processing → failed → processing in one request; the pre-check refuses while WooCommerce's stock management is switched off (stock 1 would not be enforced) and when the lines add up to more than the columns hold; a bare `<` in a name (`<2"`) no longer cuts the name or drops the line, and names with `&` reach the customer and the theme as typed; the forum list and the list show the price the product charges when it differs from the stored total.
