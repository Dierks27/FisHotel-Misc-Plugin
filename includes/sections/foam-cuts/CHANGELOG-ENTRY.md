Added Foam Cuts section — a cut sheet for the insulation foam that lines every shipping box, under FisHotel Tools → Foam Cuts (`manage_woocommerce`). **Settings**: the sheet as bought (96 × 48 in), its thickness (0.5 in), the blade's own width lost per cut (0), whether a piece may be turned 90 degrees (yes), the smallest leftover worth keeping (4 × 4 in); sizes typed as decimals or tape-measure fractions (`11 3/4`), kept as whole ten-thousandths of an inch so cuts that should meet do meet. **Boxes** come from ShipTracker's standard boxes when it has some (`FST_Settings::get_standard_boxes()`), else the Packing section's list while that section is switched on, else this section's own list; the screen says which and what was not available; filter `fishotel_foam_cuts_boxes`. **Cut list per box**: six pieces suggested from the inside dimensions and the thickness t — top and bottom L × W, long sides L × (H − 2t), short sides (W − 2t) × (H − 2t) — which the owner replaces with the list the pieces are really cut by (name, size, count); a box that changed size since is flagged. **Stock**: whole sheets as entries that add up (bought, corrected, taken by a plan, given back by an undo) and leftovers with a size and a label (L1, L2 … — a number is never handed out twice) that can be added, resized and removed by hand; three tables (`{prefix}fh_foam_leftovers`, `fh_foam_plans`, `fh_foam_sheet_log`) through dbDelta behind the autoloaded `fishotel_foam_cuts_db_version`, written only once the tables exist, all `$wpdb` in one store file. **A plan** for "this many of each box" — typed, or filled in from the orders that ship on a day (read through `wc_get_orders()` / `get_meta()`, HPOS on or off; their box from the Packing section's `fishotel_packing_confirmed_box()` / `fishotel_packing_suggest()`; without that section the screen says so and the numbers are typed): every piece placed with straight edge-to-edge (guillotine) cuts only, into the smallest free rectangle that holds it — leftovers and the offcuts of what is already being cut first, a whole sheet only when nothing else fits —, the larger remainder kept as large as possible, the kerf taken out of the remainder, pieces turned only when allowed, no cut longer than a sheet is wide unless a piece is; a deterministic search over a few orders and rules keeps the best layout; a piece that fits no sheet is reported; every layout is re-checked by replaying its cuts before it is shown. Per source (a leftover's label, or "new sheet 1"): the ordered cuts in plain words ("Cut 1: straight across the whole sheet, top to bottom, 12 in from the left edge."), the pieces with box and name, the new leftovers with the labels to write on them, the waste. **The printable page** (admin-only, nonce'd, one source per page, landscape): an SVG drawing to scale with every piece labelled, the cuts numbered in order, leftovers hatched and labelled, then the pieces per box. **Mark as cut** books the plan against the stock once (one transaction, the status moved with a WHERE on the old one) and refuses a plan made for stock that has since changed; **Undo** puts everything back while nothing else has touched what the plan touched. REST for the assistant that prints the day's paperwork: `GET fishotel-misc/v1/foam-cuts/plan/<id|latest>` (the plan as JSON — schema `fishotel-foam-cuts/plan@1`, rectangles and cuts in inches per source, ready for a CNC export one day — plus a `print_url` that opens in that user's browser), `GET …/plans`, `POST …/plan` (a draft from today's orders or typed numbers; never touches the stock), with cookie + nonce or an application password. No e-mail, no HTTP request, nothing written to an order; an ordinary front-end request reads one autoloaded option and runs no query. Headless PHP tests for the units and the suggestion, the packer (no overlaps, inside its source, guillotine, kerf, turning, leftovers first, determinism, hundreds of pieces), the plan, the stock (apply once, undo, failures half-way), boxes and orders, the SVG, the pages' and routes' permissions.
