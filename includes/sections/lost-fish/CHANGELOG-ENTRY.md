Added Lost Fish section — what happens when a fish somebody already paid for is lost. When a loss is logged on a tank group (the Clean Room now fires `fishotel_cleanroom_event_recorded` after every recorded event — one line in its `Store::record_event()`), the cover is worked out for that kind of fish (the group's product; without one, its species and size): COVERED = the fish of that kind still alive across every open group; COMMITTED = the lines of paid, not yet shipped, not closed orders that are owed that fish — paid by the paid date or `is_paid()`, never the status name; not completed / shipped / cancelled / refunded and not a combined-box wrapper; not a line a ShipTracker shipment names; the line found by its product or variation, by the Invoices section's link to the product, the species and size or the tank group (`fishotel_invoices_for_order()` / `fishotel_invoices_lines_for_product()` when present), or — a product-less fish line out of an invoice — by its name, marked "check". Covered: one line in the section's log ("replaced from the extra"), nobody is written to. Short: one case per holder line, the most recently paid order first, for as many fish as that line is short; the owner can give an unsent case to another holder, and can open one by hand from the *Open a case* tab or the link under each line of the order screen. Undoing the loss closes its unsent cases by itself; a sent one is flagged. A loss from a workbook import and a group still to be identified open nothing; two losses of one kind in the same second are worked out one after the other (a database lock). **FisHotel Tools → Lost Fish** (`manage_woocommerce`; dark, with the count of cases that need the owner on the menu): *Needs you* / *Waiting for the customer* / *Finished*, each case with what was paid for the fish, the shipping difference, the money for every choice, when the rest of the order ships, a preview of the e-mail in a sandboxed frame, and ONE button, **Send to customer** — nothing is sent without that click unless Settings → *Send by itself after the undo window* is switched on (off by default; then only a case found by its product, with a worked-out difference, while the tanks are still short). **The money** (whole cents): refund = credit = the fish minus the shipping difference (never negative), try again moves nothing and leaves the order's shipping as it is, cancel = everything paid and not yet refunded (offered only while nothing of the order has shipped). **The shipping difference** is never a number in code: the order's lines are put back into the package the checkout priced (an itemised invoice as the one invoice product it was), the order's own shipping method and instance in the zone of its address prices it as placed and — only when that gives what was charged, to the cent — without the fish; an order shipped free on purpose (every line in a "free" class, a setting; or riding in another order's box) has none; anything else — rates changed since, the method gone or needing a cart, a product gone, another case of the same order still waiting — is "not known": the reason is shown, the field must be filled or confirmed as zero, and until then Send is disabled and refused. **The e-mail** (`wp_mail()` from the store's sender; an editable plain-text template with placeholders, the store's e-mail header and footer when WooCommerce is there): which fish, when the rest ships (the checkout's `_fishotel_shipping_date` through the theme's helper and the day before it — or "we will confirm your ship date"), and the four choices as links with their money and the sentence that makes the shipping plain; one reminder after N days (2), then the case waits under *Needs you*. **The customer's page** (no login; `?fh_lost_fish=` with an HMAC-signed, expiring token bound to one case and one choice, salted per case): GET shows the same words and money and changes nothing, only the Confirm's POST records, once; reopening a case kills its links; a bad token is a 404 that names nothing. **After the answer** the case says what to do, with the amount and the link, and does none of it — refund on the order, credit to the wallet (the Batch Manager's wallets screen when active), cancel the order, or try again, which is handed to `fishotel_requests_create()` when the Requests section is there (else a reminder) — then **Done** with an optional note; the answer can also be recorded by hand. A private order note at each stage (opened, sent, reminded, the choice, done, reopened, closed), the section's own Log tab, and the Health log. Two tables (`{prefix}fh_lostfish_cases`, `fh_lostfish_log`) through dbDelta behind the autoloaded `fishotel_lostfish_db_version`, written only once both tables and every column exist; every status change is one conditional UPDATE, so a second click or a second Confirm does nothing. Orders are read and noted through the WooCommerce order API (HPOS on or off), with one read of the order-item tables to find which open orders hold the fish; an ordinary front-end request costs no query. For other sections: `fishotel_lostfish_cases_for_order()`, seven `fishotel_lostfish_case_*` actions and five filters (see the section README). Headless tests: 857 checks in 13 files — the cover maths, who is suggested, the undo, tokens, the money for each choice, every "cannot tell", the e-mail with and without a ship date, nothing sent without the click, the order notes, both contracts.
